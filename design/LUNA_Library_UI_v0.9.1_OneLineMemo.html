Uncovering Electron-Transfer Mechanisms of Sulfur Anion Photosensitizers with Intramolecular Charge Transfer

Yuki Takada, Hiroshi Nakamura, and Kenichiro Itami

ABSTRACT: Sulfur anion photosensitizers (thiolates) have emerged as powerful redox catalysts in visible-light photoredox chemistry. Here we combine spectroscopic studies, electrochemical analysis, and computational investigations to clarify the roles of intramolecular charge transfer (ICT) and photoinduced electron transfer (PET). Our results reveal important photochemical properties.

1. INTRODUCTION

Visible-light photoredox catalysis has revolutionized organic synthesis by enabling mild reaction conditions. Among various photocatalysts, sulfur anion species (thiolates) have recently attracted attention due to their strong reducing abilities and unique excited-state properties. These transformations can be applied to chemical synthesis and research.
The contribution of intramolecular charge transfer and photophysical properties is essential for the rational design of new photocatalytic systems. Recent investigations offer improved strategies and applications for organic reactions.
